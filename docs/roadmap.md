# Roadmap (high level)

Direction, not promises. Priorities shift with what users actually do.

## Now: make the coach unforgettable

- Sharpen the first-session experience: one guided action that proves the coach remembers
- Strengthen the daily two-minute rhythm
- Public tools and shareable artifacts that are useful without an account
- Search and answer-engine visibility so people can find Trifini

## Next

- **Deeper memory.** Longer-horizon recall, periodic sit-downs and annual reviews
- **Smarter proactivity.** Predictions, best-time nudges, season-aware support
- **Pet conversations.** Talk to the companion directly; cosmetics and personalisation
- **Mobile.** Wider Android release, richer widgets, iOS
- **Family.** Private coaches with an opt-in shared household layer
- **More integrations.** Wearables, banks and brokers, calendars

## Later

- **Physical robot.** Move from prototype to a small production run
- **Workplace wellbeing.** Private individual coaches for team members, with aggregate, anonymised insight for employers only
- **Wearables.** A watch surface for the companion
- **More languages and regions** beyond the current 14

## Principles that will not change

- The coach is free to talk to
- Safety features are never paywalled
- Consent before action; your data is yours to export or delete
- Respect for your time: two minutes, twice a day
